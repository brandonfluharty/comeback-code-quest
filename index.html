What we learned on the call

The shape of the system

FEED→ABSORB→ARCHITECT→DRAFT→REFINE
FEED

Your Wispr Flow voice dump per chapter. Keep this. Plus your ranked source pile, in one Project.

ABSORB

One worker reads the dump and the sources. Returns a theme map and your keeper lines. No prose yet.

ARCHITECT

Propose the chapter's shape. Name, time, situation up front. Your own words near the end. You approve before drafting.

DRAFT

Propose-only. Old line → new line → why. Never free-generate. You accept, reject, or write better.

REFINE

A cut worker removes what doesn't sound like you. A grader you don't control scores it. Then you read it out loud.

ALWAYS: contractions. Short sentences. End most paragraphs on an ordinary sentence, not a punchline. One metaphor per section at most.
